Lumenora Portfolio

Clean, Organized, & Renovated
in 1 Day.

Lumenora replaces over-engineered, $10,000+ custom contractor showroom builds with a fast, affordable, 1-day turnaround service. Customers upload photos of their cluttered garage, receive an upfront estimate, and get a completely cleared, deep-cleaned, and organized space within 24 to 48 hours of service start.

Core Differentiation

Tech-enabled pre-planning (Interactive 3D Studio + AI clutter analysis) paired with white-glove, local student-led hustle and craftsmanship.

Phase 1: Validation

Lean Launch & MVP Funding

We successfully bootstrapped the initial venture operations with a hyper-lean MVP budget of exactly $185. This seed capital funded our domain (lumenora.us), foundational cleaning supplies, and early yard sign distribution to test physical execution time and raw customer demand.

Phase 2: Productization

Digital Funnel Deployment

Transitioned from manual quoting to shipping our live 3D Garage Planner v2 and AI Clutter Scanner. The platform architecture was codified using vanilla HTML5, GSAP, and Three.js, establishing a self-serve digital funnel across the DFW Metroplex (Frisco, Plano, Prosper, McKinney, and Dallas).

Phase 3: Recognition

Uncharted Incubator Pitch Event & $1,000 Milestone

Team A.R.T successfully showcased Lumenora at the major Uncharted Incubator pitch event. We presented our 3-year revenue growth projection and secured an incredible $1,000 milestone. Check out Rishit Sachdeva's LinkedIn for the major updates and visual coverage of the event.
